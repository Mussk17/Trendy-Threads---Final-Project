# Privacy Policy (Short, Academic Demo)

## What we collect
- Account info (name, email) if you register
- Shipping/billing addresses for order fulfilment
- Order and payment metadata (processed via Stripe)
- Basic analytics events (optional)

## How we use it
- To process orders and returns
- To provide customer support
- To improve the website experience (analytics)

## Payments
- Card payments are processed by Stripe.
- We do not store full card details.

## Data retention
- Order history may be kept for accounting and customer support purposes.

## Contact
If you have privacy questions, contact support through the site’s contact form.
