# Payment Policy

## Accepted payment methods
- **Card payments via Stripe** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express where supported)
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** if enabled in Stripe settings (optional for demo)

## Currency
- Prices are shown and charged in **GBP (£)** for this academic demo.

## Payment security
- Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
- The store does not store full card numbers.

## Payment confirmation
- After successful payment, you’ll see an order confirmation screen and receive an email receipt (if email is configured).

## Failed payments
- If your payment fails, try:
  - Re-entering card details
  - Using a different card
  - Contacting your bank
- If the issue continues, contact support with your order number.
