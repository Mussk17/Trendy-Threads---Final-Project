# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Returns window
- You can return eligible items within **30 days** of delivery.

## Eligibility
Items must be:
- Unworn and unwashed
- In original condition with **tags attached**
- Returned with any included packaging/accessories

Non-returnable (for hygiene/safety):
- Items with hygiene seals removed (where applicable)
- Items showing visible wear, damage, odour, or washing

## Refunds
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method after we inspect the return.
- Inspection + processing typically takes **up to 5 business days** after the return is received.

## Exchanges
- We offer **free size exchanges (UK)** subject to stock availability.
- If the requested size is unavailable, we’ll offer a refund or store credit (your choice for this academic demo).

## How to return (academic demo steps)
1. Start a return request from your account (Order History).
2. Select the item(s) and reason for return.
3. Receive return instructions/label.
4. Send the parcel back and keep proof of postage.

## Damaged or incorrect items
- Contact support within **7 days** of delivery with photos.
- We’ll arrange a replacement or refund depending on stock and your preference.
