# Shipping Policy

## Shipping options
- **Standard (UK):** 2–4 business days after dispatch
- **Express (UK):** 1–2 business days after dispatch
- **International:** 5–10 business days after dispatch (destination dependent)

## Dispatch time
- Orders are typically dispatched within **1–2 business days** (Mon–Fri).
- Orders placed on weekends/bank holidays are processed the next business day.

## Shipping fees (example for academic demo)
- Standard (UK): **£3.99**
- Express (UK): **£6.99**
- International: **from £9.99** depending on region

## Tracking
- Tracking is provided for Express and most International services.
- Standard UK may be tracked depending on carrier availability.

## Address changes
- If your order has not been dispatched, contact support as soon as possible to request an address update.
- If the order has already dispatched, address changes may not be possible.

## Delivery issues
- If tracking shows delivered but you did not receive the parcel, check safe places and neighbours first.
- If still missing, contact support within **48 hours** so we can open a carrier investigation.
