# Materials & Care Guide

Follow these tips to keep products looking great longer.

## Cotton
- Wash cold (30°C), inside-out
- Avoid high heat drying to reduce shrinkage
- Iron on medium if needed

## Linen
- Cold wash, gentle cycle
- Hang dry; warm iron while slightly damp
- Natural creasing is normal

## Viscose
- Gentle cold wash
- Hang dry; steam to reshape
- Avoid wringing (can distort fibres)

## Wool blends
- Air between wears
- Brush lint with a clothing brush
- Dry clean for best results (follow product label)

## Recycled polyester / nylon outerwear
- Wash cold, gentle cycle
- Line dry (or low tumble where stated)
- Avoid ironing on shell fabrics
