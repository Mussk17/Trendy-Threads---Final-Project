# Size Guide (Apparel)

This guide helps you choose the right size across Tops, Bottoms, and Outerwear.

## How to measure
- **Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
- **Waist:** Measure around your natural waist (narrowest point).
- **Hips:** Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Keep the tape snug but not tight. For best results, measure over light clothing.

## Body measurement ranges (cm)
| Size | Chest (cm) | Waist (cm) | Hips (cm) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| XS | 82–86 | 62–66 | 86–90 |
| S  | 87–91 | 67–71 | 91–95 |
| M  | 92–96 | 72–76 | 96–100 |
| L  | 97–103 | 77–83 | 101–107 |
| XL | 104–110 | 84–90 | 108–114 |

## Fit types (how to interpret fit notes)
- **Fitted:** Closer to the body. If between sizes, consider sizing up.
- **Regular:** Standard fit. Choose your usual size.
- **Relaxed:** More room for comfort/layering. If between sizes, size down for a closer fit.

## If you’re between sizes
- Want a **closer fit** → choose the **smaller** size
- Want a **relaxed fit** or **layering room** → choose the **larger** size
