# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Shipping
**Q: How long does delivery take?**  
A: UK Standard is typically 2–4 business days after dispatch; UK Express is 1–2 business days; International is usually 5–10 business days.

**Q: Do you provide tracking?**  
A: Tracking is provided for Express and most International services. Standard UK may be tracked depending on the carrier.

## Returns & refunds
**Q: What is your return window?**  
A: You can return eligible items within 30 days of delivery.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**  
A: After we receive and inspect the return, refunds are typically processed within 5 business days.

**Q: Can I exchange for a different size?**  
A: Yes—free size exchanges are available in the UK (subject to stock).

## Products & sizing
**Q: I’m between two sizes—what should I do?**  
A: Check the product’s “Fit & sizing” section. For a close fit, choose the smaller size; for layering or a relaxed fit, choose the larger size.

**Q: Are your sizes UK sizes?**  
A: Yes, the size guide uses UK-centric body measurements. If you use EU/US sizes, use the conversion table in the Size Guide.

## Payments
**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: Card payments are processed via Stripe. Apple Pay / Google Pay may be available if enabled.

## Warranty / faults
**Q: What if an item is faulty or incorrect?**  
A: Contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we’ll arrange a replacement or refund.

## Support
**Q: How do I contact support?**  
A: Use the site’s contact form and include your order number if you have one.
